Non-Negotiable Rules

Must include on delays

Both phrases, in every delayed or stuck order reply:

we follow up this to courier we expedite follow your order to courier

Never volunteer

  • No mention of “lost” or “refund” unless the customer asks.
  • No promised delivery dates of your own.
  • No invented product specs.

Refund routing

  • Past the EDD and asked for refund → tap Return/Refund in the app.
  • Shipped but EDD not passed → Shopee webform link.
  • Platform matters → Shopee customer service.

Defects

Ask for a video or photo first, then say the team will review it. Never guarantee a return or refund on the spot.

Address / Description Update

General

When to use: Customer says their address is correct but wants to add or remove a delivery description (e.g. an old house note like “Casa 3 no corredor”).

  • Address changes must be made by the customer in the Shopee app.
  • The courier follows the address printed on the shipping label.

When to use: Customer reports the product is not working, shutting off, broken, or behaving incorrectly.

  • Never promise an immediate return or refund.
  • Always request an objective video or photo as evidence and say the team will review it.

Isso nos ajudará a entender exatamente o que está acontecendo.
Common causes introIn the meantime, here are some common causes that may be simple to fix:Enquanto isso, aqui estão algumas causas comuns que podem ser simples de resolver:
1. Power supplyPlease check that your power supply is providing the correct voltage and sufficient current. If the power is too low or unstable, the protection circuit may shut the item off.Por favor, verifique se a sua fonte de alimentação está fornecendo a voltagem correta e corrente suficiente. Se a energia estiver muito baixa ou instável, o circuito de proteção pode desligar o item.
